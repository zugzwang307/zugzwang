ABOUT

A mobile educational platform featuring step-by-step interactive makeup practice and personalized learning tracks tailored specifically for the Asian beauty market.

Context & Challenge

Business Goal: Conceptualize and validate a micro-modular EdTech mobile app teaching makeup techniques, tailored to the behavioral patterns of Asian audiences.

User Pain Points: Long, unstructured video tutorials caused disengagement; learners lost track of brush techniques and abandoned lessons due to lack of step-by-step practice pacing.

Constraints: Tight 6-week turnaround, solo end-to-end design across iOS/Android, and preparing a developer-ready component design system.

Strategy & MVP Scoping

Deliberately cut from MVP: Removed the in-app social feed, live 1-on-1 expert video calls, and complex competitive gamification to maintain pure focus on learning retention.

Core Loop: 3-question skill assessment → Micro-lesson flow: "Video Step → Auto-Pause → Practice" → Milestone check-in tracked on personal progress path.

Execution & UX Architecture

Key Flows: Engineered an interactive video player with smart auto-pauses after critical brush movements, paired with a 3-tier difficulty model (Beginner / Intermediate / Pro).

System Scalability: Built a modular mobile UI library in Figma with atomic step blocks, responsive video player controls, and comprehensive interaction states.
