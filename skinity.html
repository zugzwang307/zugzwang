SOLO PRODUCT DESIGNER

ROLE

MAIN IMPACT (5 WEEKS)

ABOUT

A high-tech international marketplace for buying, selling, and instantly trading in-game skins, featuring seamless Steam API integration and secure fintech transactional logic.

Context & Challenge

Business Goal: Design from scratch and launch a scalable gaming marketplace combining complex financial withdrawal logic, a digital asset catalog, and instant trades via Steam API.

User Pain Points: Competitor platforms were overloaded with chaotic data tables and intrusive banners: gamers were confused by deal steps, faced high interface friction during Steam Trade URL binding, and feared losing funds.

Constraints: Solo product design across Web Responsive, massive real-time data arrays (thousands of items), and strict UI conservatism expected by core gaming audiences.

Strategy & MVP Scoping

Deliberately cut from MVP: Postponed complex 3D case-opening animations, custom mini-games, and heavy clan leaderboards to maintain total focus on rock-solid transaction reliability.

Core Loop: Steam Auth → Instant item discovery with Float & rarity filters → Secure escrow checkout or swap → Automated Steam trade delivery.

Execution & UX Architecture

Onboarding & Activation: Designed a step-by-step onboarding wizard for Steam account binding and Trade URL verification with real-time validation, eliminating initial entry friction.

Key Flows: Established a crisp visual hierarchy for item cards (rarity color-coding, Float wear indicators, live pricing trends) and built a dedicated, ergonomic Admin Workspace for rapid item moderation.

System Scalability: Developed a dark-themed Figma design system with tokenized rarity palettes, interactive transaction patterns, and detailed handoff specifications for frontend engineers.

RETROSPECTIVE

What worked: A familiar, dark-mode layout focusing on transaction clarity and structured item cards validated the business model and drove explosive early adoption.

Next in v2.0: Refactor the UI kit toward a high-end Sci-Fi brutalism aesthetic with custom neon micro-interactions and predictive pricing charts.
