URBAN MOBILITY & GEO

PROJECT NICHE

SOLO PRODUCT DESIGNER

ROLE

2-TAP ROUTE START

MAIN IMPACT

ABOUT

A crowdsourced urban mobility mobile application designed for cyclists, enabling real-time road condition feedback, smart infrastructure-based route navigation, and community-driven hazard reporting.

Context & Challenge

Business Goal: Empower municipal cyclists to navigate safely while generating structured urban infrastructure data for city planning initiatives.

User Pain Points: Standard navigation apps ignore bike lanes, steep elevation, and road surface hazards; reporting issues while riding was dangerous due to small touch targets.

Constraints: High-contrast outdoor legibility, one-handed interaction ergonomics while cycling, and rapid 4-month end-to-end design delivery.

Strategy & MVP Scoping

Deliberately cut from MVP: Deferred complex social leaderboards and bluetooth hardware sensor pairing to focus entirely on route speed and rider safety.

Core Loop: One-tap destination search → Infrastructure route preference (Safe / Fast) → Active navigation with voice alerts → Quick hazard confirmation.

Execution & UX Architecture

Key Flows: Designed 48px+ thumb-friendly touch targets with high-contrast UI mode to ensure effortless hazard logging without taking eyes off the road.

System Scalability: Created dynamic map marker components and structured feedback survey cards supporting multi-city rollout.

2 TAPS

Frictionless route launch from lockscreen widget directly to turn-by-turn mode

48PX+

Enlarged tap zones compliant with outdoor riding ergonomics and gloved use

1 CLICK

Instant road hazard logging reducing rider distraction during active commute

RETROSPECTIVE

What worked: High-contrast outdoor map styling and oversized action targets made navigation reliable under direct sunlight.

Next in v2.0: Add automatic elevation compensation routing for e-bike commuters and municipal open-data sync.
