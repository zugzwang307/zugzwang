P2P FINTECH & MARKETPLACE

PROJECT NICHE

SOLO PRODUCT DESIGNER

ROLE

8 MIN → 90 SEC

MAIN IMPACT

ABOUT

An online P2P transaction platform and escrow marketplace designed for the US automotive market, connecting buyers, verified sellers, and managers in a transparent deal environment.

Context & Challenge

Business Goal: Eliminate fraud, hidden fees, and drop-offs during high-ticket P2P auto trades while establishing clear dispute resolution mechanisms.

User Pain Points: Users felt anxious about transferring large funds directly to private sellers; manual listing forms took up to 8 minutes with high abandonment rates.

Constraints: US financial compliance standards, multi-role transaction architecture (Buyer, Seller, Escrow Manager), and responsive web constraints.

Strategy & MVP Scoping

Deliberately cut from MVP: Postponed complex multi-tier auto auctions and third-party financing integrations to prioritize instant escrow security.

Core Loop: Step-by-step vehicle valuation → Escrow agreement setup → Real-time milestone verification → Instant payout release.

Execution & UX Architecture

Key Flows: Re-architected item listing from a tedious single-page form into a 90-second guided wizard with smart input auto-fill and clear fee breakdown.

System Scalability: Engineered a unified status tracker component supporting 3 distinct user role perspectives without duplicated layouts.

90 SEC

RETROSPECTIVE

What worked: The guided listing wizard and clean, noise-free checkout eliminated trust barriers for US buyers and sellers.

Next in v2.0: Introduce automated dispute categorization using LLM triage and expand seller analytics dashboards.
