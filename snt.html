ABOUT

A specialized SaaS B2B2C ecosystem digitizing governance for non-profit gardening communities, connecting cooperative heads, accountants, and land owners through legally binding automated workflows.

Context & Challenge

Business Goal: Automate debt tracking, legal voting, and membership fee payments while ensuring zero friction for elderly users with low digital literacy.

User Pain Points: Paper-based record keeping caused massive financial opacity, disputes over unpaid utility bills, and failed voting quorums.

Constraints: Strict regional legal compliance (Federal Law 217-FZ), WCAG AA accessibility standards, and legacy desktop browser compatibility.

Strategy & MVP Scoping

Deliberately cut from MVP: Decoupled the experimental "SNT-Bazaar" marketplace module after discovery to focus resources strictly on billing stability.

Core Loop: Automated utility statement generation → SMS/Email debt notice → 2-click web payment → Instant digital receipt & ledger update.

Execution & UX Architecture

Key Flows: Created an accessible ledger interface with 18px+ base body fonts, high-contrast tables, and foolproof 3-step digital voting verification.

System Scalability: Structured a comprehensive design system for administrative data tables, dynamic modal forms, and exportable PDF report generators.

100% LAW

Full compliance with state non-profit community regulations and legal voting quorum

2 CLICKS

Frictionless utility payment flow optimized for non-tech-savvy resident demographics

WCAG AA

High-contrast accessibility standards guaranteeing readability across all age groups

RETROSPECTIVE

What worked: Stripping out complex nested menus in favor of a flat bill payment flow allowed older residents to pay fees with zero support calls.

Next in v2.0: Integrate automated smart utility meter IoT gateways for instant telemetry and zero-human billing.
